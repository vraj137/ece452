# Team Contract

## Roles

- Akshat Jawne is our team lead and is responsible for coordinating the project.
- Vraj Bhavsar is the technical lead.
- Vishvam Patel and Edmond Yang lead the front-end design (Design Leads).
- Eric Zhu and Raghav Verma lead the back-end design.

---

## Communication

- Our team will use Discord group chat to communicate project-related matters.
- We will meet every week at 6 PM EST.

---

## Meetings

- All members will attend these meetings or notify the team in advance of anticipated absences. We all agree that absences should be rare.
- All group members will come to the meetings prepared by:
  - reading the assigned material (as much as possible), and
  - coming with ideas pertaining to the tasks and decisions to be made.
- Members will not work on other assignments during meetings and will be engaged.
- Decisions can be made during meetings even some members are absent.
- Each member will take turns listening as well as talking, and active listening will be a strategy for all group discussions.

- Akshat Jawne will post the agenda for the week in our group chat each Monday.
- Vraj Bhavsar will take notes during all meetings.
---

# Expectations

## General Expectations

- All members are expected to contribute consistently.
- Members should complete assigned tasks before deadlines.
- Members should communicate blockers early.
- Code contributions should follow agreed coding standards.

## Attendance Expectations

- Members should attend all scheduled meetings.
- If unable to attend, notice should be given in advance whenever possible.

---

# Workflow

- All code changes should go through pull requests before merging.
- At least one other team member should review pull requests when possible.

---

# Conflict Resolution

If conflicts arise, the team agrees to:

1. Discuss issues respectfully as a group.
2. Focus on solving the problem rather than blaming individuals.
3. Use compromise and collaboration whenever possible.
4. Escalate unresolved issues to course staff only if necessary.

---

# Decision Making

- Major project decisions should involve input from the full team.
- Decisions will generally be made through consensus.
- In urgent situations, the team lead may make temporary decisions after consulting available members.

---

# Agreement

By contributing to this project, all team members agree to follow the expectations and responsibilities outlined in this contract.
